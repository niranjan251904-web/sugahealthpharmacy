Legal & Clinical Policy

Terms of Medication Dispensing & Service

Effective Date: 16 November 2026 · Licensed Dispensing Facility TN-PH-82914

1. Clinical Scope & Telehealth Prescriptions

All prescription medication orders processed through the Suga.Health central vault pharmacy originate from verified clinical telemedicine consultations conducted by registered medical practitioners. The dispensing pharmacist verifies patient identity, dosage schedules, contraindications, and reported drug allergies prior to batch release.

2. Cold-Chain Compliance & Thermal Integrity

Therapies requiring temperature management, specifically Tirzepatide subcutaneous pens (Mounjaro and Yurpeak), are stored continuously between 2°C and 8°C. These shipments are dispatched in insulated thermal containment units containing calibrated phase-change gel packs. Upon delivery, the recipient is required to transfer the medication into residential refrigeration immediately.

3. Patient Responsibilities & Allergy Disclosure

Patients are legally and medically obligated to disclose known medicinal allergies, active prescriptions, and cardiovascular or endocrine conditions during their physician intake. Suga.Health is not liable for complications arising from unstated contraindications.

4. Discreet Shipping & Packaging Protocols

To respect patient privacy, medications in sensitive therapeutic categories, including sexual health treatments (Sildenafil, Tadalafil, Dapoxetine), are packed in unmarked, opaque outer cardboard boxes. Exterior documentation carries strictly logistical manifests without medical indications.

5. Tamper-Evident Seals & Cancellation Policy

Due to good distribution practice (GDP) standards, pharmaceutical items with broken tamper-evident seals cannot be returned or re-entered into inventory. If a package arrives visibly damaged or showing temperature excursion flags, patients must report the issue within 24 hours for inspection and immediate replacement.
