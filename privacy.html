Prescription Data & Health Privacy Policy

Compliance Framework: Telemedicine Practice Guidelines & Digital Personal Data Protection Regulations

1. Scope of Collected Clinical Data

Suga.Health collects medical information strictly necessary for physician evaluation, prescription issuance, and pharmaceutical dispensing. This includes patient contact numbers, shipping addresses, physician consultation summaries, dosage schedules, reported allergy profiles, and dispensing batch numbers.

2. Pharmacist Audit Trails & Access Controls

Access to individual patient prescription logs is restricted to licensed pharmacists (such as Chief Pharmacist Rahul Patel, Lic. TN-PH-82914) and verified prescribing clinicians. Every stock verification, batch allocation, and status update is logged with a permanent cryptographic timestamp.

3. Courier Partner Data Minimization

When an order transitions from the pharmacy into the shipping section, integrated third-party logistics partners (Blue Dart Express Health, Delhivery Healthcare) receive only the recipient name, delivery address, and contact number. Clinical diagnoses, specific treatment names, and dosage regimens are never disclosed to courier personnel.

4. Telemetry & Cold-Chain Sensor Logs

For medications requiring temperature-controlled delivery (Tirzepatide), environmental sensor data measuring package interior temperature is recorded solely to verify storage compliance during transit and is retained for quality assurance.

5. Patient Data Rights

Patients have the legal right to request an export of their complete dispensing history, update contact information, or request data archiving in accordance with healthcare record retention regulations.
